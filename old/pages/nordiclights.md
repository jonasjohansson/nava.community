[NORDIC LIGHTS](https://nordiclights.live/) is a union of three emerging audiovisual festivals on a mission to explore culture synergies through experimental collaboration. Through an open call with 119 submissions, 12 artists were selected and paired into 6 new AV acts.

Supported by Nordic Culture Point and Nordic Culture Fund.
