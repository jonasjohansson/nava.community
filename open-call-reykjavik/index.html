Reykjavik 2019
25—27 April

Announcement ⚡

With 124 applications sent in we are happy to announce the selected artists: Giovanni Muzio (IT), Line Finderup Jensen (DK), Tirador (ES), Van Ta (ES) and Jana Spacelight (DE).

With the announcement of the Sónar Reykjavik cancellation we are co-hosting an alternative experience with an incredible lineup — more info TBD.

NAVA is again inviting artists to perform live visuals (VJ) and participate in the annual meetup. Selected artists will be paired with music acts for a collaborative and unique performance.

Join the team and help create this years audiovisual experience in Reykjavik!
