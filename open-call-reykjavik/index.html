Sónar Reykjavik 2019
25—27 April

Announcement ⚡

With 124 applications sent in we are happy to announce the selected artists: Giovanni Muzio (IT), Line Finderup Jensen (DK), Diego López Bueno (ES), Marcos Gonzalez (ES) and Jana Spacelight (DE).

With the announcement of Sónar Reykjavik cancelling we are co-hosting an alternative audiovisual experience with an incredible lineup. More info TBD.

NAVA is again inviting artists to perform live visuals (VJ) at the festival and participate in the annual meetup. Selected artists will be paired with booked music acts for a collaborative and unique performance.

Join an International team and help co-create this years audiovisual experience at the Reykjavik Harpa concert hall!
