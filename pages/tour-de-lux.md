Tour de Lux is a rolling parade of projections, where custom built Beamer Bikes (off-grid bikes for projections) provide the canvas for audiovisual performances and more.

With support from [Nordisk Kulturfond](https://www.nordiskkulturfond.org/) and the [Opstart fund](https://nordiskkulturfond.org/opstart-info/) NAVA is hosting an open call for building the bike (and again later on for content). Our motivation is to:

-   **Connect** nordic countries through a creative challenge
-   **Create** great open source projection bike designs
-   **Present** mezmerising experiences outside of black and white boxes
-   **Inspire** those that are not normally exposed to audiovisual culture

Each design unit will receive a budget to build the bike. In the spirit of DIY it is encouraged to upcycle, collaborate and reuse/rent/borrow rather then purchase new.

## How does it work

### Apply

-   [Application form](https://forms.gle/2XvdTabLoxXWZw2D9)
-   Deadline: 17th of August 2020, 24:00 CET

We are looking for designers, makers, creatives who can by themselves or (preferably) in a small team produce a bicycle capable of mobile outdoor projections.

### Compensation

The design teams will share a total budget of **€2500** for production. There is no additional monetary compensation. It is up to the teams to distribute the funds, but expenses must be communicated.

NAVA will provide the framework, assist with reaching out to technical partners (projectors), facilitate the work process, set up digital infrastructure and provide marketing material.

### Schedule

-   20.07.27 Open call start
-   20.08.17 Open call end
-   20.08.24 Bike build begin

_Presentation of project happens during autumn/winter 2020_

### FAQ

_What is a Beamer Bike?_

Not sure if that name exists, but what we mean are bikes with space for projector, computer and battery. [Watch this video of VJ Suave](https://vimeo.com/141932372) for a better understanding.

_What happens when the bike is built?_

Together we will share a common vision regarding the content to be presented, a discussion which all design teams are welcome to participate in. We will then host an open call for content to be screened. Content can come from many places, from collaborations with schools to short films. It can be games, or live audiovisual content. We decide.
