Tour de Lux is a rolling parade of projections, where custom built Beamer Bikes (off-grid bikes for projections) provide the canvas for audiovisual performances, games and more. With support from [Nordisk Kulturfond](https://www.nordiskkulturfond.org/) and the [Opstart fund](https://nordiskkulturfond.org/opstart-info/) NAVA is hosting two open calls, the first for building the bike, and the latter for content. Together we hope to create a _Bright Bike Society_ and:

-   **Connect** nordic countries through a creative challenge
-   **Create** great open source projection bike designs
-   **Present** mezmerising experiences outside of black and white boxes
-   **Inspire** those that are not normally exposed to audiovisual culture

Each design unit will receive a budget to build the bike. In the spirit of DIY it is encouraged to upcycle, collaborate and reuse/rent/borrow rather then purchase new.

NAVA will provide the framework, assist with reaching out to technical partners (projectors), facilitate the work process, set up digital infrastructure and provide marketing material.

### Compensation

Three design teams each receive a budget of **700 EUR** to produce the bike. There is no additional monetary compensation. It is up to the teams to distribute the funds, but expenses must be communicated.

### Schedule

-   20.07.27 Open call start
-   20.08.17 Open call end
-   20.08.24 Build begin

_Presentation of project happens throughout October and November_

### FAQ

_What is a Beamer Bike?_

Not sure if that name exists, but what we mean are bikes with space for projector, computer and battery. [Watch this video of VJ Suave](https://vimeo.com/141932372) for a better understanding.

_Why is the monetary compensation not higher?_

This is a pilot project, and funds are limited. We hope to make the best with what we have, and apply for a larger grant in the future. Thank you for understanding.
