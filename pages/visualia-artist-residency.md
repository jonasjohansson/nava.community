Artist residencies give space for artistic exploration, intimate collaboration and professional reflection - and was a natural complement to participation in large events. Continuing the recipe of combining audio and visuals, Visualia invited artists [Sol Sarratea](https://solquemal.com/) and [Brenda El Rayes](https://www.instagram.com/brendakakor/) to co-create an audiovisual experience in the Swedish countryside.

{% fig "visualia-residency-04.jpg"  %}

The residency is hosted by [Jonas Johansson](https://jonasjohansson.se) at his childhood home in Långaveka, which used to function as a school of the village children.

To learn more about the residency, visit [www.visualia.se](https://visualia.se/).

{% fig "visualia-residency-05.jpeg", "Image by Ola Folkesson, HN.se."  %}

Visualia artist residency is made possible through [support from Region Halland](https://www.regionhalland.se/nyheter/region-halland-ger-kulturstod-till-25-projekt/).
