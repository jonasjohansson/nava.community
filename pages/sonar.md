## Open call

NAVA is inviting 4 acts to perform live visuals during the festival and actively participate in the first meetup. Selected acts will be paired with sound artists / musicians for a collaborative performance.

Come and join an International team of artists and help co-create this years audiovisual experience!

### Apply

-   Application deadline: 8th of February 2018, 24:00 CET
-   [Application form](https://goo.gl/forms/YAKwDcxLeSoLgzUI3)

Read the [FAQ](https://goo.gl/B1wG6m) for questions and leave a comment for new inquiries.

### Compensation

-   Artist honorarium 100€ + Travel grant 100€
-   Festival pass
