NAVA and Fotografiska hosted the first [AV meetup](https://www.facebook.com/events/2719615168268380/) at Studio Live, the in-museum audiovisual hangout.

The evening included an introduction by Britt van der Poel (Music Experience Manager, Fotografiska) and Jonas Johansson (NAVA), and was followed by local artists:

-   [Anton Heestand](http://hexagons.se/), [Pixel Nodes](https://pixelnodes.app/)
-   [Half Scheidl](https://hscheidl.com/), Generative Art with the [Axidraw](https://www.axidraw.com/)
-   [Nadia Capo Woutuk](https://nadiacw.com/), [Rethread](https://rethread.art/) and Making Sense of Software through Art
-   Christophe Abé, IllumiNoir
-   Erik Natanael, Real-time AV as Instrument Augmentation
-   Petter Sundqvist ([Videoapelsin](https://videoapelsin.tv/)), [Mandelbulb3D](https://www.mandelbulb.com/)
-   Jonas Hermann, [StageCast](http://stagecast.io/)/LiveHacks
