Fotografiska
26 February, 2020

NAVA and Fotografiska hosted the first AV meetup at Studio Live, the in-museum audiovisual hangout.

The evening included an introduction by Britt van der Poel (Music Experience Manager, Fotografiska) and Jonas Johansson (NAVA), and was followed by local artists:

  • Anton Heestand, Pixel Nodes
  • Half Scheidl, Generative Art with the Axidraw
  • Nadia Capo Woutuk, Rethread and Making Sense of Software through Art
  • Christophe Abé, IllumiNoir
  • Erik Natanael, Real-time AV as Instrument Augmentation
  • Petter Sundqvist (Videoapelsin), Mandelbulb3D
  • Jonas Hermann, StageCast/LiveHacks
