CCTV
6 March, 2021

Carbon Copy TV by Martin Kling is an audiovisual concept, documenting and developing the cultural scene in Stockholm. In his own words "Hearing and seeing is equally important in CC experiences. Its coherence is space and time. What is seen shall be as enjoyable as what is heard."

For the launch of the CCTV headquarters NAVA will curate the Saturday visual experience. Expect to see some Visualia superstars in this 1-day rollercoaster of visual weirdness!
