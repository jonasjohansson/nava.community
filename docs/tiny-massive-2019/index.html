Tiny/Massive 2019
Reykjavik Winter Lights Festival
7–10th February

With 58 beautiful and diverse projects it was a difficult task to find those we collectively deemed as outstanding. We initially planned to only offer 3 artists the travel grant but ended up with 4! Special salutations to DICE by Joohyun Park, Dongphil Yoo and Youjin Chung (US), Mountain of Light by Damien Seguin (FR), Tempest by Matt DesLaurier (CA) and Water & Light by CS Wallace, Jack Schlesinger and Nolan Filter (US).

Honorary mention to TinySpaceGame by Tomi Mäkinen (FI) which definately got the peoples choice award judging by their enthusiam playing.

This was an incredible project for us to lead and be a part of. We spent a considerable amount of time preparing a Creators Kit for artists to work with, and led a course at Iceland University of Arts where student teams would create interactive audiovisual work later presented during the festival. We were blown away by the amount of people who made it over to visit the event, and so we prepared a meetup where we got to meet and learn from each other. Thank you all who made this project a reality!
