Visualia
11 September, 2020

25 metres below the surface Reaktorhallen will again become an experimental test facility as audiovisual performances and installations take space. Visualia is a single-day evening event co-created by and for artists.

Performances will be made by Martin Kling / PRO424, Virtuoso, ssusui / Concat Function, Jaime Reyes & Håkan Lidbo (Rumtiden), petit sapin / Memory Lake , Niklas Alriksson (Glimt) / Brenda El Rayes, Servando Barreiro / Joakim Hellgren, Spherical Aberration / SASTHOR, Away From People / Skaggis, Hexagons / P A U. & YXA and MAGMA present 11 unique AV performances.

Installations by Torbjörn Fernström and David Carjaval.

We are opening up the possibility to present AV Installations by using the Application form. Deadline 31st of August. Applications will be reviewed as they come.

We are using this Tech-kit from Smart Kreativ Stad and a Kvant FB4 Laser is provided by Laserboy to those that wish to use it.
