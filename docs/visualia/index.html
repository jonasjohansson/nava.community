Visualia
11 September, 2020

Smart Kreativ Stad announced the possibility to rent their Tech-kit at a reduced price. NAVA, in collaboration with Adam Siversen Ljung, is inviting visual artists and electronic music artists to perform at a special location in Stockholm. Selected artists will be paired for a collaborative and unique performance.

If you are unsure whether you are right for this open call, please fill out the form regardless and we will get in touch. This is open for everyone, and depending on the replies we are open for surprises.

  • Deadline: 3rd of August 2020, 24:00 CET

Compensation

There is no monetary compensation in this event. In return, we will provide you with a stage, and documentation of the event and performance.

Schedule

Location

We are looking at locations ideal for projections in Stockholm but have yet to decide on the main contender.

Important
