Tour de Lux

Tour de Lux is a rolling parade of projections, where bikes are upgraded with a projector and battery in order to present urban audiovisual screenings and improvisational stages.

With support from Nordisk Kulturfond and Opstart, NAVA is enabling design teams in Stockholm (Carl Edwin Waerner), Oslo (Rasmus Stride), Helsinki (Jan-Erik Lindroos) to construct their bike, and final experience.
