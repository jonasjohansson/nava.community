Tour de Lux

Tour de Lux is a rolling parade of projections, where moving vehicles are upgraded with a projector and battery in order to present urban audiovisual screenings and improvisational stages.

With support from Nordisk Kulturkontakt and Opstart, NAVA is enabling design teams in Oslo, Helsinki and Stockholm to construct their AV mobile machines.

Helsinki

Jan-Erik Lindroos and Pyöräpaja organised Freaky Bike Parade, a constellation of projections, lights and custom bikes!

Stockholm
