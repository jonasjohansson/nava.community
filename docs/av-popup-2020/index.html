AV Popup 2020
11 September

Open call

Smart Kreativ Stad announced the possibility to rent their Tech-kit at a reduced price. We seized the moment.

NAVA and Teckmantel are inviting artists to perform live visuals (VJ) in Stockholm. Selected artists will be paired with music acts for a collaborative and unique performance.

The music acts will be announced along with the visual artists.

Apply

There is no monetary compensation in this event. In return, we will provide you with a beautiful stage, and documentation of the event and performance.
