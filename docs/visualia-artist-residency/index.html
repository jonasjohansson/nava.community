Visualia Artist Residency
20 September, 2021

Artist residencies give space for artistic exploration, intimate collaboration and professional reflection - and was a natural complement to participation in large events. Continuing the recipe of combining audio and visuals, Visualia invited artists Sol Sarratea and Brenda El Rayes to co-create an audiovisual experience in the Swedish countryside.

The residency is hosted by Jonas Johansson at his childhood home in Långaveka, which used to function as a school of the village children.

To learn more about the residency, visit www.visualia.se.

Visualia artist residency is made possible through support from Region Halland.
