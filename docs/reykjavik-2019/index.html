Reykjavik 2019
25–27 April

With 124 applications sent in we are happy to announce the selected artists: Tirador (ES), Van Ta (ES) and Jana Spacelight (DE).

With the announcement of the Sónar Reykjavik cancellation we are co-hosting the unique audiovisual experience Skaðablót at Bíó Paradís together with Plútó.
