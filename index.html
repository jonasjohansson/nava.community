NAVA

Nordic Audiovisual Artists (NAVA) is a community for artists working in the intersections of music and visual art. It will take its first physical form during Sónar Reykjavik 2018 where invited artists, together with the NAVA team, will outline a framework for the community's existence.

Open call

Sónar Reykjavik 2018
16th — 17th March

NAVA is inviting 4 artists to perform live visuals, as well as actively participate in the first meetup.

Apply

  • To participate, artists have to be resident of either Nordic countries: Denmark, Finland, the Faroe Island, Greenland, Iceland, Norway, Sweden and the Aaland Island
  • Application deadline: 8th of February
  • Application form

We welcome submissions that fit the following criteria

  • Work that utilises the close relationship between image with sound
  • Projects or work submitted must be recent

Submissions will be judged on the basis of

  • Originality
  • Audience appeal
  • Technical logistics and costs

Please note

  • Short films and installation based work are not currently being supported in our open call
  • All video links should be a maximum of 15 minutes
  • All images should be no smaller than 1920px x 1080px

Payment

  • Artist honorarium 100 Euro + Travel grant 100 Euro
  • Full festival pass for all participants

Accomodation/Storage

  • Invited artists must by own means find accommodation for the period of their stay, NAVA will however provide highly affordable options.
  • NAVA will provide free and spatious options for storage but without any claim of responsibility

FAQ

Can I apply as a team? Yes. However, you will not receive additional payment.
My nationality is different from the countries mentioned, can I still apply? Yes, but you must be a resident of either FI, NO, SWE, IS or DK.
